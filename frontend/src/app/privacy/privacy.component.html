Hyderabad Urban Realty ("we", "our", "us") operates hydurbanrealty.onrender.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our platform to explore RERA-registered properties in Hyderabad.

By using our service you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use.

1. Information We Collect

Account registration (email/password):

  • Full name, email address, optional mobile number
  • Password (stored as a BCrypt hash — we never store plain-text passwords)

Account registration (Google Sign-In):

  • Name, email address, and profile photo provided by Google after your explicit consent
  • Google subject ID (used solely to identify your account on future sign-ins)
  • We do not receive your Google password

Usage data: Pages visited, search queries, properties saved or compared. This data is used only to improve the platform — never sold.

Voluntary submissions: Property enquiry forms, site-visit requests, resale listings, and reviews include contact details you provide.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and personalise the platform (saved searches, favourites, property comparisons)
  • Send transactional emails — account verification, password reset, review notifications
  • Connect interested buyers with property enquiries (WhatsApp / email forwarding)
  • Comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud

We do not use your data for advertising, sell it to third parties, or share it without your consent except as described below.

3. Google Sign-In & OAuth Data

When you choose "Continue with Google" we receive a one-time ID token from Google. We verify this token server-side, then extract only your name, email address, and profile picture URL (if available). We store your Google subject ID to recognise your account on future sign-ins.

We do not access your Google contacts, Drive, Calendar, Gmail, or any other Google service. Our Google OAuth scope is limited to openid profile email.

Your use of Google Sign-In is also governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Sharing of Information

  • Supabase — our cloud database and file-storage provider. Data is stored encrypted at rest.
  • Resend — transactional email delivery. Only your email address and the email body are shared.
  • Google — only when you choose Google Sign-In (see §3 above).
  • Law enforcement — only if required by a valid legal order.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser localStorage to store your JWT session token so you stay logged in across page reloads. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or analytics pixels. You can clear localStorage at any time in your browser settings.

6. Data Security

Passwords are hashed with BCrypt (cost factor 12). All API communication is over HTTPS/TLS. Access tokens expire after 15 minutes and are rotated on every refresh. We run regular dependency audits.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at the address below. Deletion requests are processed within 30 days. Note that deletion is permanent and will remove all saved properties, reviews, and listings associated with your account.
