About the Consultant

Welcome to Hyderabad Urban Realty – your trusted partner in finding premium real estate in the vibrant city of Hyderabad. Founded by Ravi, a seasoned Civil Engineer with over 12 years of international experience in the real estate sector, our company is built on a foundation of expertise, passion, and a commitment to sustainable development.

That background shapes how every project gets read here — not through a sales lens, but through the questions a structural engineer asks before committing: What is the build quality? Is the floor plate well-designed? What does the promoter's track record actually say?
